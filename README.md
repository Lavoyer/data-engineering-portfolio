# Data engineering portfolio

Case studies from production work: scheduled data jobs, the platform that runs them, and the systems those jobs call.

The pages stay at architecture, versions, and engineering choices.
They leave out employer identifiers, source code, internal names, and business rules.

## Cases

- [Orchestrating data automations with Airflow 3](projects/airflow-orchestration/README.md) — 42 production DAGs for collection, calculation, integration, reporting, and approval.
- [Infrastructure and observability](projects/infrastructure-observability/README.md) — two Docker Compose hosts, centralized logs, and a watchdog that alerts Microsoft Teams.
- [Integrations](projects/integrations/README.md) — HTTP, Microsoft 365 mail, SharePoint, and browser automation.
- [Portfolio monitoring API](projects/portfolio-monitoring-api/README.md) — holdings grouped by class and subclass, with three limit layers and a business-day restatement. Co-developed with another engineer on a small team.
- [Efficient frontier](projects/efficient-frontier/README.md) — a custom NSGA-II front under per-name and per-group bounds. Co-developed with another engineer on a small team.
